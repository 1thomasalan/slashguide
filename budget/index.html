/budget

Builds a simple monthly budget from your income and costs.

The full prompt

/budget Build a simple monthly budget. Take-home pay: [amount]. Regular costs: [list each cost and amount]. Goal: [e.g. save $300 a month].
Show a table with needs, wants, and savings, with totals and what's left over. Show your math. Then suggest 3 realistic places to trim, without judging my choices.

Replace the highlighted [brackets] with your own details, then send.

What you're asking

You ask for a monthly budget from your numbers. You get a table, totals, and a few ideas to save.

Give it

  • Take-home pay
  • Each regular cost
  • Your savings goal
  • Irregular costs (gifts, car repairs)

Check: Double-check the totals; AI can make math mistakes. This isn't financial advice.

Filled-in example

/budget Build a simple monthly budget. Take-home pay: $4,200. Regular costs: rent $1,500, car payment $350, insurance $180, groceries $500, utilities $220, phone $80, streaming $40, eating out $250. Goal: save $400 a month.
Show a table with needs, wants, and savings, with totals and what's left over. Show your math. Then suggest 3 realistic places to trim, without judging my choices.
