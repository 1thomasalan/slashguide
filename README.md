# Slash Guide

Plain-English slash shortcuts for AI chat and everyday apps (Slack, Notion, Discord, Obsidian, Teams, Copilot). A One to Better project: https://onetobetter.com

- The repo root is the published static site (GitHub Pages serves it). Everything outside `sitegen/` and `editions/` is generated; don't edit it by hand.
- `sitegen/` holds the generator, styles and script. `sitegen/apps_data.py` lists the built-in app commands, each section linked to the vendor's own help page. `sitegen/shorts.py` maps a shortcut to its YouTube Short.
- `editions/` holds the shortcut data shared with the free PDF editions.

No ads, trackers, analytics or cookies. Shortcuts your AI understands are words it reads as intent, not official features; results vary a little by model. Not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, Microsoft, GitHub, Slack, Notion, Discord or Obsidian.
