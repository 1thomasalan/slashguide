/scale

Resizes a recipe up or down to the servings you need.

The full prompt

/scale Change this recipe from [current] servings to [new] servings: [paste the recipe]. Show the new ingredient list with amounts rounded to easy kitchen measures. Then note anything that may not scale simply, like pan size, cooking time, eggs, or baking.

Replace the highlighted [brackets] with your own details, then send.

What you're asking

You ask the AI to resize a recipe. You get new amounts plus notes on what to adjust.

Give it

  • The full recipe
  • Current and new servings
  • Your pan or pot size

Check: Check the math on key amounts, and check food is cooked through, since bigger batches can take longer.

Filled-in example

/scale Change this recipe from 4 servings to 10 servings: [paste your chili recipe]. Show the new ingredient list with amounts rounded to easy kitchen measures. Then note anything that may not scale simply, like pan size, cooking time, eggs, or baking.
