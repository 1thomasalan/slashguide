/insurance

Explains health insurance terms and walks through a bill or statement.

The full prompt

/insurance Explain this health insurance question in plain words: [term, or paste the bill or statement with personal info removed].
Define any insurance terms in one or two sentences each, with a simple dollar example. If I shared a bill, walk through each charge line by line and show the math step by step so I can check it.
End with 2 or 3 questions I could ask my insurer.

Replace the highlighted [brackets] with your own details, then send.

What you're asking

You ask for insurance terms or a bill explained. You get plain definitions, step-by-step math, and questions for your insurer.

Give it

  • The term, or the bill's lines and amounts
  • Your plan's deductible and copays if you know them
  • What confuses you

Check: Check the math yourself and confirm coverage with your insurer; your plan documents are the final word. Remove your name, member ID, and account numbers before pasting.
