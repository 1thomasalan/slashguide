/dispute

Drafts a polite message disputing a wrong charge.

The full prompt

/dispute Write a short, polite message to [company or bank] disputing a charge. Charge: [amount] on [date]. Why it's wrong: [reason]. What I want: [refund, correction].
Include a subject line, the key facts in 2 or 3 sentences, a clear request, and a list of documents I should attach. Keep it under 150 words and firm but friendly.

Replace the highlighted [brackets] with your own details, then send.

What you're asking

You ask for a dispute message about a charge. You get a ready-to-send note and a list of proof to attach.

Give it

  • The amount and date
  • Why it's wrong
  • What you want
  • Booking or account number

Check: Check facts before sending, keep copies, and note any deadline your bank or card has for disputes.

Filled-in example

/dispute Write a short, polite message to Harbor View Hotel disputing a charge. Charge: $189 charged twice on May 3. Why it's wrong: I stayed one night only, booking #HV22917. What I want: a refund of the duplicate $189.
Include a subject line, the key facts in 2 or 3 sentences, a clear request, and a list of documents I should attach. Keep it under 150 words and firm but friendly.
