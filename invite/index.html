/invite

In everyday life

Writes an invitation for a party, dinner or event.

Add

What, when, where, and anything guests should bring.

Example

/invite 60th birthday BBQ, Sat June 6, 4 pm, our backyard

Works in

  • ChatGPT
  • Claude
  • Gemini
  • Copilot
  • Grok

A shortcut your AI understands from the word, not an official feature. Results vary a little by model.

In Slack

Adds someone to a channel.

Add

@someone and, if it's another channel, #channel.

Example

/invite @sam #project-launch

Works in

  • The Slack message box
