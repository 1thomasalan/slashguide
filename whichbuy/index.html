/whichbuy

Helps you compare devices that fit your needs and budget.

The full prompt

/whichbuy Help me choose a [type of device]. I'll use it for [uses]. Budget: [amount]. Must-haves: [features]. Already own: [devices or brand].
First list the 4 or 5 features that matter most for my uses. Then suggest 2 or 3 options in a short table (price range, pros, cons) and say which you'd pick and why. Say if your product info may be out of date.

Replace the highlighted [brackets] with your own details, then send.

What you're asking

You ask for help choosing a device. You get key features to look for and a short comparison.

Give it

  • What you'll use it for
  • Your budget
  • Devices you already own
  • Must-haves

You need: Current models and prices work best with an AI that can search the web.

No web access? Use the feature list to compare current models in a store or online.

Check: Check current prices, models, and return policies before buying.

Filled-in example

/whichbuy Help me choose a tablet. I'll use it for reading and FaceTime with family. Budget: under $400. Must-haves: easy to see screen. Already own: an iPhone.
First list the 4 or 5 features that matter most for my uses. Then suggest 2 or 3 options in a short table (price range, pros, cons) and say which you'd pick and why. Say if your product info may be out of date.
