/fridge

Suggests meals from food you already have.

The full prompt

/fridge Here's what I have: [attach a photo of your fridge, or list the items]. Suggest [3] meals for [number] people that I can make in [time] using mostly these items. For each, list anything extra I'd need and short steps. If you can't tell what something is in the photo, ask me.

Replace the highlighted [brackets] with your own details, then send.

What you're asking

You ask the AI for meal ideas from what's on hand. You get a few quick options with steps.

Give it

  • What food you have
  • How many people
  • How much time
  • Allergies or dislikes

You need: Photo option needs an AI app that accepts images (most do, some only on paid plans).

No photo upload? Just type a list of what you have.

Check: Check that leftovers and fresh items are still good to eat.

Filled-in example

/fridge Here's what I have: eggs, half a bag of spinach, cheddar, 2 potatoes, an onion, leftover rice. Suggest 3 meals for 2 people that I can make in under 30 minutes using mostly these items. For each, list anything extra I'd need and short steps. If you can't tell what something is in the photo, ask me.

Use it in any AI chat. The slash word is a label, not a built-in command; the prompt does the work, and answers vary by app. How these work
