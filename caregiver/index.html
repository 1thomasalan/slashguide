/caregiver

Drafts a care checklist or weekly plan for a loved one.

The full prompt

/caregiver Help me organize care for [who] who [situation]. What's hard right now: [challenges].
Make a weekly checklist grouped by daily, weekly, and as-needed tasks (meals, medicines, appointments, home safety, and time for me to rest).
Keep it short and printable. Add a short list of questions to ask their care team. Follow the care team's instructions over anything here.

Replace the highlighted [brackets] with your own details, then send.

What you're asking

You ask for help organizing a loved one's care. You get a printable checklist and questions for their care team.

Give it

  • Who you're caring for and their situation
  • What's hardest right now
  • Appointments and helpers involved
  • Care-team instructions

Check: Follow the doctor's and care team's instructions first; this is for organizing, not medical advice.

Filled-in example

/caregiver Help me organize care for my mom who is home after hip surgery. What's hard right now: keeping track of her pills and getting her to physical therapy twice a week.
Make a weekly checklist grouped by daily, weekly, and as-needed tasks (meals, medicines, appointments, home safety, and time for me to rest).
Keep it short and printable. Add a short list of questions to ask their care team. Follow the care team's instructions over anything here.
