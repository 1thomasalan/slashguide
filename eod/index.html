/eod

End of Day

Writes a short end-of-day update from your notes.

The full prompt

/eod Write my EOD (end-of-day) update for [who]. Done today: [list]. Still in progress: [list]. Blockers or help needed: [list]. Up next: [list]. Format: [number] short bullets, plain and professional.

Replace the highlighted [brackets] with your own details, then send.

What you're asking

You ask the AI to turn your notes into an end-of-day update. You get a few clean bullets.

Give it

  • What you did
  • What's next
  • Blockers
  • Who reads it

Check: Read it before sending.

Filled-in example

/eod Write my EOD (end-of-day) update for my manager. Done today: sent signage proofs, fixed app banner. Still in progress: newsletter draft. Blockers or help needed: waiting on event photos from the program. Up next: finish newsletter tomorrow. Format: 3 short bullets, plain and professional.

Use it in any AI chat. The slash word is a label, not a built-in command; the prompt does the work, and answers vary by app. How these work
