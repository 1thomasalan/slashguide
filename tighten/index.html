/tighten

Shortens your draft to a target length, keeping the key points.

The full prompt

/tighten Shorten the draft below to under [number] words. Keep every request, date, and number. Cut filler and repetition, keep my tone, and give me only the revised text.

Draft:
[paste your draft]

Replace the highlighted [brackets] with your own details, then send.

What you're asking

Asks the AI to cut your text down without losing what matters. You get the shorter version only.

Give it

  • Your draft
  • Target word count
  • Anything that must stay

Filled-in example

/tighten Shorten the draft below to under 100 words. Keep every request, date, and number. Cut filler and repetition, keep my tone, and give me only the revised text.

Draft:
Hi team, I just wanted to reach out and touch base about the upcoming open house. As you may know, we have been planning this for quite a while now, and I think it is going to be a great event. I wanted to ask if everyone could please send me their volunteer availability by next Wednesday, March 4, so that I can put together the schedule. Also, please remember that the setup starts at 8 a.m. Thanks so much for all your help, I really appreciate it.
