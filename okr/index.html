/okr

Objectives and Key Results

Turns a goal into objectives and measurable key results.

The full prompt

/okr Turn my goal into OKRs (Objectives and Key Results: an inspiring objective plus 3 measurable results that show we got there). Goal: [goal]. Time frame: [quarter or dates]. Starting point: [current numbers]. For each key result give a number, a deadline, and how we will measure it. Ask me for the starting numbers if I did not give them.

Replace the highlighted [brackets] with your own details, then send.

What you're asking

You ask the AI to turn a goal into OKRs. You get an objective and measurable results with targets.

Give it

  • The goal
  • Time frame
  • Current numbers

Filled-in example

/okr Turn my goal into OKRs (Objectives and Key Results: an inspiring objective plus 3 measurable results that show we got there). Goal: grow downloads of our app. Time frame: January to March. Starting point: about 2,000 downloads a month. For each key result give a number, a deadline, and how we will measure it. Ask me for the starting numbers if I did not give them.

Use it in any AI chat. The slash word is a label, not a built-in command; the prompt does the work, and answers vary by app. How these work
