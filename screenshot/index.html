/screenshot

Explains what's on your screen and what to tap next.

The full prompt

/screenshot Look at this screenshot from my [device]. Explain in plain words what it is showing and what it wants me to do. Then tell me exactly what to tap next, step by step. If it looks like a scam or a warning, say so.
[upload screenshot, or type exactly what the screen says]

Replace the highlighted [brackets] with your own details, then send.

What you're asking

You ask the AI to read your screen and tell you what to do. You get a plain explanation and the next taps.

Give it

  • The screenshot or exact words on screen
  • Your device
  • What you were trying to do

You need: An AI app that accepts photos (most do, some only on paid plans).

No image upload? Type exactly what the screen says, including button names.

Check: Cover personal info (names, account numbers) before sharing a screenshot.

Filled-in example

/screenshot Look at this screenshot from my iPhone. Explain in plain words what it is showing and what it wants me to do. Then tell me exactly what to tap next, step by step. If it looks like a scam or a warning, say so.
The screen says: "iCloud storage is full. Upgrade now or manage storage." with buttons "Upgrade" and "Not Now".
