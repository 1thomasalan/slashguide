/swot

Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats

Runs a quick SWOT check: strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats.

The full prompt

/swot Do a SWOT analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) for [project or idea] in this context: [context]. Give 3-4 specific points in each of the four boxes, based on what I have told you, and mark guesses as assumptions. End with the top 2 actions it suggests.

Replace the highlighted [brackets] with your own details, then send.

What you're asking

Asks the AI for a four-part strategic check. You get a SWOT grid plus two suggested actions.

Give it

  • The project or idea
  • Your team, budget, and audience
  • Competitors or alternatives
  • Your goal

Filled-in example

/swot Do a SWOT analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) for launching a member app next spring in this context: we run community programs for about 20,000 members, have a small web team of 3, and members mostly find events through social media. Give 3-4 specific points in each of the four boxes, based on what I have told you, and mark guesses as assumptions. End with the top 2 actions it suggests.

Use it in any AI chat. The slash word is a label, not a built-in command; the prompt does the work, and answers vary by app. How these work
