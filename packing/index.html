/packing

Makes a packing checklist for your trip.

The full prompt

/packing Make a packing checklist for [number] days in [place] in [month]. Bag: [e.g. carry-on only]. Activities: [what you'll do]. Group it by clothes, toiletries, documents, tech and health, with quantities, and keep it light enough for my bag.

Replace the highlighted [brackets] with your own details, then send.

What you're asking

You ask the AI for a packing list that fits your trip and bag. You get a grouped checklist with amounts.

Give it

  • Where, when and how long
  • Bag size
  • What you'll be doing
  • Medicines or special needs

Check: Check your airline's size and weight limits and liquid rules. Keep medicines and documents in your carry-on.

Filled-in example

/packing Make a packing checklist for 10 days in Italy in May. Bag: carry-on only. Activities: city walking, churches, and a nice dinner or two. Group it by clothes, toiletries, documents, tech and health, with quantities, and keep it light enough for my bag.

Use it in any AI chat. The slash word is a label, not a built-in command; the prompt does the work, and answers vary by app. How these work
