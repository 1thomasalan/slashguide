/faq

Frequently Asked Questions

Turns a long document into questions and answers.

The full prompt

/faq Turn the document below into an FAQ (frequently asked questions) for [audience]. Write [number] questions they would really ask, each with a short plain-English answer taken only from the document. If the document does not answer an obvious question, list it at the end under 'Not covered'.

Document:
[paste document]

Replace the highlighted [brackets] with your own details, then send.

What you're asking

Asks the AI to rewrite a document as questions and answers. You get an FAQ plus a list of gaps.

Give it

  • The document
  • The audience
  • How many questions

You need: Long documents may be more than some AI apps take in one message.

Too long? Paste it in parts, then ask for one combined result at the end.

Check: Have the policy owner review answers before you share them.

Filled-in example

/faq Turn the document below into an FAQ (frequently asked questions) for staff. Write 8 questions they would really ask, each with a short plain-English answer taken only from the document. If the document does not answer an obvious question, list it at the end under 'Not covered'.

Document:
Effective Jan 1, all employees earn 4 hours of annual leave per pay period. Up to 240 hours may carry over to the next year. Leave requests over 3 days need 2 weeks' notice. Sick leave of more than 3 days requires a doctor's note. Unused leave above the cap is lost on Jan 1.

Use it in any AI chat. The slash word is a label, not a built-in command; the prompt does the work, and answers vary by app. How these work
