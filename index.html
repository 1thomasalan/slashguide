How the AI shortcuts work

  1. They're prompts, not commands. No AI app has a built-in /eli5. The slash word is a label you can remember; the words after it tell the AI the job. That's why every page gives you the full prompt.
  2. Fill in the [brackets]. The details you add are what make the answer good. Each page lists what to give.
  3. If a menu pops up when you type /, that's the app's own tool list (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Microsoft 365 Copilot do this). Press Esc or tap away and keep typing, or paste the whole prompt in one go.
  4. Photos and files need an app that accepts uploads. Most do, often with limits on free plans. Every page that needs one says so and gives a type-it-in option.
  5. Check what matters. AI can be confidently wrong. Check facts, numbers and anything about health, money or the law before you rely on it.

Obsidian

Official list

Typing / opens a search of Obsidian's commands. Type a few letters and press Enter. These aren't fixed commands; they're the words that find each one.

Obsidian matches loosely, like its Command palette, so a few letters find any command. Press Esc or Space to close the search.

  • /tableFinds Insert Table, which adds a Markdown table.
  • /calloutFinds Insert callout, which adds a note callout or wraps your selected text in one.
  • /templateFinds Templates: Insert template. Needs the Templates core plugin.
  • /dateFinds Templates: Insert current date. Needs the Templates core plugin.
  • /todayFinds Open today's daily note. Needs the Daily notes core plugin.
  • /propertyFinds Add file property, for tags, dates and other note details.
