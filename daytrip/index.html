/daytrip

Suggests day-out ideas from where you are.

The full prompt

/daytrip Suggest [3] day trips from [your town] in [month or season]. Interests: [e.g. history, nature, good lunch]. Max travel each way: [e.g. 1.5 hours by car]. For each, give the travel time, what to see, a lunch idea, and a simple timeline for the day.

Replace the highlighted [brackets] with your own details, then send.

What you're asking

You ask the AI for day-out ideas near you. You get a few options, each with travel time and a plan for the day.

Give it

  • Your starting town
  • The season or date
  • Interests
  • How far you'll travel

You need: Current hours and events need an AI app that can search the web.

No web search? Use the ideas as a starting point and check each place's website.

Check: Check opening hours and any seasonal closures before you set off.

Filled-in example

/daytrip Suggest 3 day trips from Tampa in October. Interests: history and a good lunch. Max travel each way: 1.5 hours by car. For each, give the travel time, what to see, a lunch idea, and a simple timeline for the day.

Use it in any AI chat. The slash word is a label, not a built-in command; the prompt does the work, and answers vary by app. How these work
