/rewriteforaudience

Rewrites a passage for a different audience and reading level.

The full prompt

/rewriteforaudience Rewrite the text below for [audience] at about a [grade] reading level. Keep every fact and requirement, and do not add new ones. Use short sentences and a [tone] tone. After the rewrite, list anything you removed or simplified.
Text: [paste text]

Replace the highlighted [brackets] with your own details, then send.

What you're asking

You ask the AI to rewrite text for a new reader. You get the rewrite plus a note of what was simplified.

Give it

  • The text
  • The audience
  • Reading level and tone

Check: Check policy rewrites with the policy owner before sharing.

Filled-in example

/rewriteforaudience Rewrite the text below for frontline staff at about an 8th-grade reading level. Keep every fact and requirement, and do not add new ones. Use short sentences and a friendly tone. After the rewrite, list anything you removed or simplified.
Text: All personnel shall ensure that facility access credentials are displayed at all times while on the premises and that any loss thereof is reported to the security office within 24 hours.
