/scamcheck

Points out scam warning signs in a message or call.

The full prompt

/scamcheck Check this message for scam warning signs. List each warning sign you see in plain words, say how risky it looks (low, medium, high), and tell me what to do next. Remind me never to click links or call numbers in the message; contact the company using its official website or app instead.
Message: [paste the text or describe the call; remove any personal info]

Replace the highlighted [brackets] with your own details, then send.

What you're asking

You ask for a scam check on a message or call. You get the red flags, a risk level, and safe next steps.

Give it

  • The exact text or what the caller said
  • Who it claims to be from
  • What it asks you to do

Check: The AI can't confirm a message is safe. Don't click links; contact the company through its official site or app. Never share codes or passwords.
